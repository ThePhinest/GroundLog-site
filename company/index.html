What we do

Software for the people who walk the site

Environmental inspectors on construction projects carry a permit's worth of duties in their heads and a day's worth of evidence in their pockets. The daily log, the stormwater inspection, the photo record, the compliance entries and the open acreage count all describe the same day, and most of the time they are kept in five different places.

GroundLog keeps them in one. What is entered in the field once is what the inspection, the daily report and the project record all read from. The inspector writes it down a single time, and it carries all the way up. We call that from boots to boardroom.

GroundLog is built by a working environmental inspector and used on active construction sites. The features come from problems met on real jobs.

How we work

Privacy comes first
